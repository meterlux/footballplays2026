# Decitrig Slide Rule

A touch simulator of the K&E 4081-3 Log Log Duplex Decitrig (20-scale version), built for iPhone.

- **Learn**: 15 guided lessons with "More" pop-ups, "Show me" demos and hands-on "Your turn" checks
- **Practice**: 14 problem types with hints and animated walkthroughs
- **Test**: 10 timed problems, scored for slide-rule accuracy, with best scores saved
- **Expert**: full-screen rule only

Gestures: drag the glass (or the body) to move the whole rule, drag the middle strip to move the slide, flick sideways quickly to flip the rule over, pinch to zoom, and tap a scale name to see what it does. Dragging is speed-sensitive: slow movement is geared down to about 1/3 for fine settings, normal speed is 1:1, and fast strokes are geared up (to 3.5×) so one stroke can cross the rule.

The rule's surface, stains, slide ends and aluminum end brackets come from photos of the actual rule (`textures.js`): perspective-corrected, with the printed ink and the cursor removed. The scales are drawn on top mathematically, so every reading is exact.

It is two files (`index.html` and `textures.js`). On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and it runs full screen.
